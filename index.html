Privacy Policy – QR Code and Barcode Scanner

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This policy explains how the QR Code and Barcode Scanner app ("the app"), published by Waqar Naeem, handles your information.

Camera

The app asks for camera access only to scan QR codes and barcodes. Camera frames are processed on your device in real time. They are never saved, uploaded or shared.

Photos

If you choose to scan a code from a photo, or add a logo to a QR code you create, the app reads only the image you pick. The image is processed on your device and is not uploaded. When you save a generated code, it is saved to your device's gallery.

Scan and generate history

The codes you scan and create, and your settings, are stored only on your device. You can delete individual items, clear the whole history, or turn history saving off in Settings. Uninstalling the app removes this data. If you have Android backup turned on, this data may be included in your device's own backup to your Google account, which is managed by Google under your backup settings.

Third-party services

The app uses Google ML Kit to recognise barcodes. Recognition happens on your device, but ML Kit may send limited diagnostic and usage information (such as device and app information and performance metrics) to Google to keep the service working and improve it. This is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. See ML Kit's data disclosure for details.

What we do not do

  • We do not require an account or collect your name, email or contacts.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information.
  • We do not run our own servers that receive your data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
